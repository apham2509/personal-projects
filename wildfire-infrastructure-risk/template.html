Provisional vs confirmed

History up to late April 2026 is the calibrated standard-processing archive, which classifies detection types and filters out static industrial heat. The most recent weeks come from the near-real-time feed: available within ~3 hours of overpass, but provisional - no type classification yet, so industrial sources can appear until the archive catches up.

Why the type filter matters

In early validation the most "exposed" asset in Iberia was the port of Gijón, with 180 days of apparent fire exposure. Every one of them was the neighbouring steelworks: a persistent thermal anomaly that looks identical to fire in a single observation. The archive's vegetation-fire classification removes it - the single most consequential data-quality decision in this pipeline.

Current alert tiers

Rule-based and fully explainable: a score from recency of the last detection within 10 km, latest distance, latest-day fire radiative power, consecutive active days, and activity versus the asset's own seasonal norm. High is 70+, Elevated 40+, Watch 15+. No black box.

Fire incidents

Detections are clustered into distinct incidents: same ~5 km neighbourhood, active within 3 days of each other. 100 detections can be one persistent fire observed repeatedly - incidents say which.

Chronic exposure

score = 0.5 × exposure-day percentile + 0.35 × cumulative-FRP percentile + 0.15 × proximity factor, computed over the full 2018-now record. It measures how often and how intensely fire has come near an asset - it does not yet include asset criticality or disruption outcomes.
