## Common questions

**My cat triggered the exit screen / cannot exit.** The play screen exits
only when both top corners are held for two seconds and the owner check is
confirmed (press-and-hold, or your PIN if you set one). To fully lock the
device to PawSense, use iPad Guided Access or Android app pinning —
explained in Settings > Safety guidance.

**Where is my data?** Entirely on your device. Settings > Data management
exports everything as JSON or CSV and offers deletion of any scope. There
is no account to recover and no server copy — if you delete data, it is
gone.

**Sound is not playing.** Check the session-setup sound toggle and the
app-wide setting; cats marked "easily startled" always start silent by
design (change it in the cat's profile if appropriate).

**A session ended by itself.** PawSense ends sessions gently when the cat
loses interest for 30 seconds or shows repeated frustration signals, and
always at the planned duration (5 minutes maximum). The results screen
shows which of these happened.

**Insights say "no conclusion yet".** Claims appear only with enough
evidence (at least 8 comparable targets, more for stronger labels). Play a
few more sessions.

## Reporting a bug

Include: device model, OS version, app version (Settings > About), what
you did, and what happened. If it concerns a specific session, the session
date from History helps. Exports contain no personal information beyond
what you typed into profiles, so attaching a JSON export is often the
fastest route to a fix.
