# PawSense Privacy Policy

Effective date: to be set at first store release. Version 1 (matches
`privacyPolicyVersion` in the app).

## The short version

PawSense stores everything on your device. We do not collect, transmit,
sell, or share any data. There is no account, no cloud service, no
advertising, and no analytics.

## What the app stores (on your device only)

- Cat profiles you create: name, questionnaire answers, optional notes,
  optional photo.
- Play data: sessions, individual targets, paw-touch positions (as screen
  fractions), and the preferences PawSense learns from them.
- Optional voice cue recordings you make (for example "Touch" or "Good").
- Your settings, including an optional owner PIN stored only as a salted
  hash.

All of this lives in PawSense's private app storage. It is never uploaded
by the app. Deleting the app deletes all of it; the app also provides
in-app deletion of any session, any cat, or everything at once, effective
immediately.

## Permissions

- Microphone: used only while you record a voice cue; recordings stay on
  the device. The app works fully without this permission.
- Photos: PawSense uses your device's photo picker, which shares only the
  single image you choose.

PawSense requests nothing else and contains no third-party advertising or
analytics software.

## Export and sharing

You can export your data (JSON or CSV) from Settings > Data management.
Exports are handed to your device's share sheet; where they go is entirely
your choice. Exports do not include photo or audio files.

## Children

PawSense is made for cat owners and does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone — it cannot, as it collects nothing.

## Changes

If a future version ever adds an online feature, it will be optional, off
by default, and this policy will be updated and shown in the app before
the feature can be used.
