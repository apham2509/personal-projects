## Long description

Every cat hunts differently. PawSense learns yours.

PawSense is a personalised enrichment and positive-reinforcement training
app for cats. Each cat gets its own profile: a short questionnaire sets
gentle starting points, a balanced calibration session observes what
actually works, and every session after that adapts — the prey, the
movement, the speed, the size — to your individual cat.

- Three original prey: a scurrying mouse, a fluttering moth, a gliding
  fish, each with smooth, stop-and-go, and unpredictable movement styles
- Touch Training: record "Touch", "Good", and "All done" in your own
  voice; PawSense cues, rewards, and signs off with them
- Honest insights: catch rates, reaction times, favourite prey and
  movement, and a paw heatmap — always with the sample size and a
  confidence label, never overclaiming
- Built calm: short sessions with a five-minute cap, soft synthesised
  sounds, no flashing, and automatic easing when play stops being fun
- Multi-cat households: individual profiles that never mix, plus a Mixed
  Session mode when everyone joins in
- Private by design: no account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. All data
  stays on your device and can be exported or deleted at any time

PawSense is enrichment, not veterinary advice. Supervise play, use a
stable stand, and finish with a physical toy your cat can really catch.

## Safety copy (store + review notes)

Sessions are capped at five minutes and end early when the cat loses
interest or shows frustration; there are no flashing effects or sudden
loud sounds; treats are optional and diet-neutral; the app supervises
towards calm, successful play and never optimises for screen time.
Reviewer note: the play screen intentionally has no visible UI; owners
exit by holding both top corners for two seconds, then confirming.

## Privacy summary (store forms)

No data collected or shared; everything is stored on-device; microphone
only while recording a voice cue; photos via the system picker; in-app
export (JSON/CSV) and full deletion. See docs/RELEASE_GUIDE.md for the
exact Apple/Google form answers.
